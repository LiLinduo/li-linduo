Sports

🧗 Rock Climbing — I enjoy rock climbing as a way to challenge both strength and focus. It is a fun activity that combines problem-solving, balance, and patience. For me, it is also a way of looking inward for strength and overcoming myself one move at a time.

🏔️ Mountain Climbing — I like mountain climbing and hiking because they offer a chance to explore nature, build endurance, and enjoy quiet moments away from daily routines. It feels like a way of looking outward for strength, drawing energy from open landscapes, distance, and the natural world.

🎱 Snooker — I enjoy snooker for its precision, strategy, and calm pace. It is a game that rewards concentration and careful planning.

🎾 Tennis — I am looking for the rhythm, movement, and competition on the tennis court. It is a sport that pushes me to read the opponent, make quick decisions, and keep improving through each rally.

🏀 Basketball — I enjoy basketball as a fast-paced team sport. It is a great way to stay energetic, competitive, and connected with others.
