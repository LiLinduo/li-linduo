Hi, I’m Linduo Li, a Master student in Data and Artificial Intelligence at Institut Polytechnique de Paris.

My research interests are mainly in natural language processing and computer vision, with a broader interest in building large language model applications for domain-specific problems. I am interested in identifying niche areas where AI systems can address concrete pain points, and in designing practical workflows that connect machine learning methods with real-world use cases.

Recently, I have worked on information retrieval for tax law , studying how retrieval systems can identify relevant legal authorities from complex, goal-oriented queries. I have also worked on archaeological site detection and recognition , where computer vision, remote sensing imagery, geospatial data, and domain knowledge are combined to support AI-assisted site discovery.

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Geophysics at Tongji University and later received my Master of Science in Data Analytics Engineering from Northeastern University. After that, I worked as a Data Scientist at Blend360, where I developed machine learning models for clients in the retail industry to solve business problems such as customer retention, attribution analysis, and data-driven decision support.

I then worked as a Large Language Model Developer and AI Engineer, focusing on the development of LLM-powered application products. These experiences shaped my interest in both research-oriented machine learning and deployable AI systems, and led me to continue my studies in Data and Artificial Intelligence at Institut Polytechnique de Paris.
