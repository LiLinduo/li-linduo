About Me

Hi, I'm Linduo Li, a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence, Systems and Data (IASD) at Université Paris Sciences et Lettres (PSL).

My research interests are mainly in natural language processing and computer vision, with a broader interest in building multimodal applications for domain-specific problems. I am interested in identifying niche areas where AI systems can address concrete pain points, and in designing practical workflows that connect AI methods with real-world use cases.

Recently, I have worked on information retrieval for tax law , studying how retrieval systems can identify relevant legal authorities from complex, goal-oriented queries. I have also worked on archaeological site detection and recognition , combining computer vision, remote sensing imagery, geospatial data, and domain knowledge to support AI-assisted site discovery.

Previously, I worked as a Data Scientist at Blend360, where I developed machine learning models for clients in the retail industry, addressing challenges such as customer retention, attribution analysis, and data-driven decision support. I later worked as a Large Language Model Developer and AI Engineer at Boston Listener, focusing on LLM-powered applications. These experiences shaped my interest in both research-oriented machine learning and practical, deployable AI systems.
